## About Me

I am a **Data Analyst** focused on transforming raw data into clear, reliable, and actionable insights. I enjoy exploring data, identifying meaningful patterns, building practical dashboards, and supporting better business decisions through evidence-based analysis.

My work combines analytical thinking, data visualization, and business understanding to communicate complex findings in a simple and useful way.
